Pressure Dynamic Par Edge vs Consistency Matrix

Benchmark of uncapped player reliability across match situations. Hover over any bubble to inspect metrics.

Why This Evaluation Framework Exists

Standard cricket averages (e.g. 35.0 average or 8.5 economy) fail in high-stakes auction war rooms because they treat low-pressure overs identically to high-pressure crunch moments.

This matrix isolates genuine clutch resilience by plotting:

  • Y-Axis (Dynamic Par Edge): The statistical gap between the player's performance and expected era/phase par. For batters, this is strike rate edge above par (death par = 151.5). For bowlers, this is runs saved per over below par (death par = 9.25 RPO).
  • X-Axis (Consistency Rating %): The proportion of crunch outings where the player produced positive match impact, punishing volatile single-match wonders.
  • Bubble Radius (Sample Weight): Scaled to the square root of high-pressure deliveries faced/bowled, ensuring battle-tested volume is prioritized.

How to Interpret the 4 Tactical Quadrants

  • Quadrant I (Top-Right) — Elite Clutch Closers: High Edge + High Consistency (≥ 60%). Priority Auction Targets. Deliver game-winning performances repeatedly under maximum leverage (e.g., Mahipal Lomror +16.8 SR Edge / 82% Cons; Jagadeesha Suchith +3.42 Saved/Ov / 100% Cons; Murugan Ashwin +1.10 Saved/Ov / 80% Cons; Kuldeep Sen +0.70 Saved/Ov / 67% Cons).
  • Quadrant II (Top-Left) — Explosive Impact Weapons: Outstanding peak boundary hitting and strike capabilities with volatile leverage demands (e.g., M. Shahrukh Khan +3.0 SR Edge / 56% Cons; Abishek Porel -0.8 SR Edge / 58% Cons).
  • Quadrant III (Bottom-Right) — Calm Par Anchors: High consistency (≥ 60%) and steady phase control, protecting against collapses (e.g., Atharwa Taide +1.7 SR Edge / 60% Cons; Praveen Dubey +0.40 Saved/Ov / 75% Cons; Simarjeet Singh +0.25 Saved/Ov / 60% Cons; Narayan Jagadeesan 67% Cons).
  • Quadrant IV (Bottom-Left) — Squad Depth Rotation: High-upside rotational options and situational specialists (e.g., Nehal Wadhera, Akash Madhwal, Rajvardhan Hangargekar, Lalit Yadav, Hrithik Shokeen, Darshan Nalkande, KM Asif).
